MRR and faithfulness were nearly unrelated (ρ = −0.0333). Evidence completeness showed a modest relationship with answer completeness (ρ = 0.3395). Twenty-six labels are human-owner scores; 144 are disclosed offline AI scores.

What results mean

Prompt-RAG led MRR and graded nDCG. Hybrid led Recall@10. BM25 remained highly competitive. FAISS and graph superiority hypotheses were not supported. No composite score or universal winner is claimed.
