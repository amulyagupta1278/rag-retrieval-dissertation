## Violation 1 — Wrong seed and wrong sampling design

`docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL_V2.md:41` states:

> Stochastic seed: 42 for every local stochastic operation.

The regrade package was generated with **seed 123** and **grade-stratified**
allocation (87/13/14 across grades 0/1/2).

Two defects, not one:

1. **Seed 123 violates the frozen protocol.** Seed 42 is mandatory.
2. **Grade-stratified sampling is not blind.** Stratifying by first-pass grade
   leaks the first-pass label into the sample design and oversamples grade 2
   (14/95 = 14.7%) relative to grade 0 (87/572 = 15.2%) by construction. An
   intra-rater agreement estimate from a grade-stratified sample is not an
   unbiased estimate of agreement over the pool, because per-grade agreement
   rates differ sharply (observed: 98.9% at grade 0 vs 50.0% at grade 2).
   The protocol requires blind simple random sampling.

**Consequence:** the reported agreement rate of 91.23%, κ=0.7718, and κ_w=0.8916
are not protocol-conformant estimates and must not be cited.

1. **Intra-rater agreement is definitionally a human self-consistency measure.**

2. **The first pass was also AI-generated.** So the 91.23% figure is an AI-vs-AI

3. **Gold-standard relevance judgments underpin every downstream metric.** H1–H4
   verdicts, all nDCG/MRR/Recall figures, and the dissertation's comparative claims

**Consequence:** the final label set, the final qrels, and every metric and
